# team-mirae

> **Designing software for tomorrow.**

## Who we are

Mirae Research is a software development busienss registered in the Republic of Korea.
We primarily develop customized groupware/collaborativity solutions tailored for specific
busiensses, designed for on-premise deployment.

### Defense in depth

No single layer of security is bulletproof. We believe that defense in depth is the best
way to secure your operations in the increasingly hostile environment of the public internet.
Cybersecurity is not black magic - anyone can secure their online operations for very little
cost, or sometimes completely for free.

### Safe and convenient

Using virtual private networks and a well-secured central authority, cybersecurity can be
both safe and convenient. We design solutions to reduce your time logging in, while making
your operations more secure. And none of it lives on our servers. Your data stays on your computers.

### Completely free

Our solutions are open-source, and completely free. Take your operations off the public internet.
Try out our suite of software now!
